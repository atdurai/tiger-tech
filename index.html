Business portfolio profile

Tiger Tech is not a generic IT-services company.

Tiger Tech helps business owners replace disconnected manual work with one simple operating system. The work starts by studying the current business process: how orders arrive, how bills are made, how stock is checked, how payments are followed up, and how reports are prepared.

The result is a custom business application designed around the real daily workflow of the company, not a generic software template.
